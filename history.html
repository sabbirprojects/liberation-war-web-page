The Road to Victory

Chronology of 1971

MARCH 7, 1971

The Historic Speech

Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman delivered a monumental address at the Racecourse Maidan, effectively calling upon the millions of Bengalis to prepare themselves for a definitive struggle for independence.

MARCH 26, 1971

Declaration of Independence

Following the brutal crackdown of Operation Searchlight by occupation forces on March 25, the formal proclamation of independence was transmitted, igniting the organized armed resistance of the Mukti Bahini.

DECEMBER 16, 1971

The Dawn of Sovereignty

The war concluded with the unconditional surrender of the occupying forces at the Ramna Racecourse. Bangladesh was liberated as a sovereign, independent nation after 9 months of supreme sacrifice.
