About the Liberation War

The Bangladesh Liberation War was a revolutionary independence movement in 1971 that led to the rise of a sovereign nation. Fueled by decades of economic, political, and cultural discrimination, the people of East Pakistan united under the leadership of Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman to fight against oppressive military forces.

National Guardians

Featured Heroes of 1971

Leadership
Sheikh Mujibur Rahman

The Father of the Nation and the supreme architect of independent Bangladesh, whose historic March 7 speech galvanized millions to fight for independence.

Bir Sreshtho
Noor Mohammad Sheikh

A Lance Naik in the East Pakistan Rifles who fought valiantly in Sector 8, sacrificing his life while providing cover fire to save his fellow soldiers.

Bir Sreshtho
Matiur Rahman

A Flight Lieutenant who attempted to defect from the Pakistani Air Force with a fighter jet to join the liberation force, dying in the attempt.

Bir Sreshtho
Hamidur Rahman

The youngest Bir Sreshtho recipient, an army Sepoy who charged an enemy machine gun nest single-handedly in Dhalai, ensuring victory at the cost of his life.

National Flag of Bangladesh

The green represents the vibrant land and hope, while the red disc symbolizes the sun of independence and the blood sacrificed by millions in 1971.

National Martyrs' Memorial

Located in Savar, *Jatiyo Smriti Soudho* features seven cascading triangular planes representing key historic milestones from 1952 to 1971.

The Freedom Fighters (Mukti Bahini)

Brave guerrilla fighters, students, peasants, and military defectors who united to form the resistance against the occupying forces.

Victory Day Celebration

Commemorating December 16, 1971, when the Pakistani military surrendered unconditionally, marking the official liberation of Bangladesh.

The Historic 7th March Speech

Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman addressing a massive gathering at the Race Course, calling on the nation to prepare for liberation.

Liberation War Museum

Preserving the tangible history, archival documents, personal belongings, and weapons used during the historic struggle of 1971.

MUKTI71

A digital archive dedicated to preserving the history, struggles, and ultimate victory of the 1971 Bangladesh Liberation War for future generations.
